# Initial Skill Catalog and Content Governance

**Status:** Implementation baseline for the MVP. Learner research may change ordering, not the content-quality standard.

## 1. Selection principles

Launch skills are prioritized by:

- practical value for Pakistani learners ages 16–30;
- suitability for five-to-ten-minute challenge sessions;
- clear, measurable learning outcomes;
- ability to create realistic scenarios and feedback;
- search and answer-engine discoverability;
- low risk of misleading professional or regulated advice;
- availability of qualified reviewers;
- content freshness and maintenance burden;
- premium-personalization potential.

SkillUp should launch with a small number of complete, reviewed paths rather than hundreds of shallow AI-generated titles.

## 2. MVP launch catalog

### 1. Interview and Workplace Communication — pilot path

**Why first:** Broad relevance, clear outcomes, strong scenario-gameplay fit and manageable review risk.

Learner outcomes:

- introduce themselves clearly;
- answer common interview questions with evidence;
- ask useful clarifying questions;
- write concise workplace messages;
- handle feedback, disagreement and follow-up professionally;
- recognize weak, vague or risky communication patterns.

Initial modules:

1. Introductions and confidence
2. Interview answers with evidence
3. Workplace messages and email basics
4. Feedback, disagreement and difficult conversations
5. Meetings, follow-up and professional reliability

Primary challenge types:

- scenario decision;
- multiple choice;
- ordering;
- fill in the blank;
- short-response practice with rubric-assisted feedback.

### 2. Practical English for Study and Work

Learner outcomes:

- improve high-frequency vocabulary and sentence clarity;
- understand common study and workplace instructions;
- write short, useful messages;
- identify frequent grammar and tone mistakes;
- build confidence through contextual practice.

This path must not promise fluency through a fixed number of levels.

### 3. AI Tools for Study and Work

Learner outcomes:

- choose appropriate AI tasks;
- write clear instructions and provide context;
- verify outputs and identify hallucinations;
- protect private information;
- use AI for study, writing, analysis and productivity responsibly;
- understand when not to use AI.

Content requires frequent review because tools, model behavior and policies change.

### 4. Freelancing Foundations

Learner outcomes:

- choose a service and define a basic offer;
- interpret a client brief;
- ask scope questions;
- write a concise proposal;
- manage expectations, revisions and handover;
- identify scams and unsafe payment or credential requests.

SkillUp must avoid guaranteed-income claims.

### 5. Digital Marketing Foundations

Learner outcomes:

- understand audience, offer, funnel and channel basics;
- distinguish organic, paid, search and social activity;
- define a simple content objective;
- interpret basic campaign metrics;
- recognize misleading claims and vanity metrics;
- create a small, measurable campaign plan.

Platform-specific content must include a review date.

Regulated, high-stakes or credential-sensitive topics—medical, legal, investment, tax, safety-critical trade instruction and formal exam certification—require a separate risk review before inclusion.

## 4. Pilot path completion standard

The pilot is complete only when it contains:

- one published skill page;
- one versioned learning path;
- at least five modules;
- at least twenty levels;
- a balanced mix of challenge types;
- explicit learning objectives for every level;
- reviewed explanations and remediation;
- a baseline assessment and end-path assessment;
- accessibility and low-bandwidth verification;
- public summary content for SEO/AEO/GEO;
- analytics for start, attempt, completion, mastery, drop-off and upgrade exposure.

The level count is a launch planning target, not a promise that all paths must use the same length.

## 5. Content roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Product owner | Approves audience, path priority, premium boundary and success measure |
| Subject author | Defines outcomes, module structure, scenarios and source material |
| AI-assisted drafter | Produces schema-conforming drafts through the approved model gateway |
| Subject reviewer | Verifies accuracy, pedagogy, difficulty and answer logic |
| Language reviewer | Reviews clarity, English usage and future Urdu translation |
| Safety/editorial reviewer | Reviews claims, bias, privacy, cultural suitability and policy risk |
| Publisher | Confirms metadata, discoverability, version and approval evidence |
| Content owner | Maintains freshness, corrections, retirement and learner-impact response |

One person may hold more than one low-risk role in the MVP, but public AI-assisted content must not be generated and approved by an unreviewed automation alone.

## 6. Content lifecycle

```text
Draft request
→ Source and learning-outcome definition
→ AI-assisted or human draft
→ Schema validation
→ Duplicate and safety checks
→ Subject review
→ Language/editorial review
→ Preview
→ Publish immutable version
→ Monitor quality and learner outcomes
→ Correct through a new version
→ Archive or retire
```

Learner attempts always reference the exact content version presented.

## 7. Source and evidence policy

Every path must state:

- source type and ownership;
- publication or access date where relevant;
- which claims require ongoing freshness review;
- whether AI transformed or summarized the source;
- reviewer identity or internal reviewer record;
- prohibited copying or licensing restrictions.

Do not fabricate sources, statistics, quotations or credentials. Public content must distinguish SkillUp guidance from external standards or official certification.

## 8. Quality rubric

A level cannot publish unless it passes:

- **Accuracy:** correct answer and explanation are defensible.
- **Alignment:** challenge tests the stated learning objective.
- **Clarity:** learner can understand the task on a small screen.
- **Fairness:** distractors are plausible but not deceptive.
- **Usefulness:** feedback explains why, not only whether.
- **Difficulty:** appropriate for the declared stage.
- **Accessibility:** interaction has keyboard and non-drag alternatives.
- **Safety:** no harmful, discriminatory or unsupported advice.
- **Originality:** not a near-duplicate or copied source passage.
- **Discoverability:** public summary is useful and not thin keyword content.

## 9. Freshness and correction

- High-change AI and platform-specific content: review at least every 90 days.
- General career and communication content: review at least every 12 months.
- Urgent factual or safety errors: unpublish or correct immediately through a new version.
- Corrections must preserve prior learner attempt history.
- Material corrections require an audit event and affected-content report.

## 10. Pilot success measures

The pilot path should be evaluated on:

- first-level start rate;
- first-level completion rate;
- module and path completion;
- correct-answer improvement between baseline and end assessment;
- explanation helpfulness;
- seven-day learning return;
- drop-off by level and challenge type;
- content-report rate;
- premium upgrade exposure and conversion without coercive prompts.
