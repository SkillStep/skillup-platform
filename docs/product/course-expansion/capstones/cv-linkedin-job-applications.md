# CV, LinkedIn & Job Applications — practical capstone project

**Draft educational asset. Status: NOT REVIEWED / NOT PUBLISHED.** 
**Course:** `cv-linkedin-job-applications` · **Wave 1** · **Target:** 24 proposed levels.
**Outcome:** Communicate verifiable strengths and tailor applications to real roles
**Practical objective:** Produce a role-targeted CV, profile summary and application tracker

## Fictional scenario

Fictional employer Example Services seeks a Junior Operations Coordinator who can maintain order trackers, write customer updates and organize weekly reports. The applicant has completed a university group project and helped a student society; no employment history should be invented.

## Starting materials and constraints

- Sample role requirements: spreadsheet tracking, professional messages, accurate data entry, collaborative handovers
- Sample evidence: organized a 12-person student event; created a 20-row fictional expenses tracker; drafted three event updates
- A blank one-page CV and a blank application tracker; use fictional addresses and contact details

## Student deliverables

- One-page accessible CV, using only the sample applicant's credible evidence
- Four-bullet role-to-evidence matching table
- A 100-word LinkedIn About draft and a truthful job-specific cover note
- Application tracker with status, next follow-up date and link fields
- A privacy check that avoids publishing phone numbers from fictional exercises

## Self-check rubric

- No invented qualifications, employers, years of experience or measurable claims
- Relevant evidence supports at least four listed job requirements
- CV hierarchy, dates and language are clear and ATS-readable
- Each message is professional, specific and free of unsupported guarantees
- Application tracker supports a realistic follow-up without harassment
