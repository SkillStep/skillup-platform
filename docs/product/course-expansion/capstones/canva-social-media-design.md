# Canva & Social Media Design — practical capstone project

**Draft educational asset. Status: NOT REVIEWED / NOT PUBLISHED.** 
**Course:** `canva-social-media-design` · **Wave 1** · **Target:** 24 proposed levels.
**Outcome:** Create accessible, consistent social visuals with clear communication
**Practical objective:** Design a five-post branded social mini-portfolio

## Fictional scenario

Fictional Naya Brew is introducing a weekend breakfast menu. Create a five-post social mini-campaign without using existing commercial logos, copyrighted photos or customer images.

## Starting materials and constraints

- Brand brief: calm, modern neighborhood café, accessible text, friendly direct language
- Draft campaign themes: announcement, menu highlight, hours, customer questions and a weekend reminder
- Use only self-created shapes, original illustrations or properly licensed assets

## Student deliverables

- Mini brand guide with two type styles, an accessible palette and spacing rules
- Five distinct 4:5 feed posts with useful readable captions
- One story variant with safe-area and contrast checks
- Source/licensing attribution notes for all outside assets, if any
- Two-sentence explanation of how the hierarchy supports each post's goal

## Self-check rubric

- Post goals and messaging differ rather than copy/paste five near-identical posters
- Text remains readable at mobile size; information is not conveyed by color alone
- Logo, font, imagery and tone remain coherent
- Posts have correct dimensions with no cropping of critical content
- Rights and accessibility checks are documented
