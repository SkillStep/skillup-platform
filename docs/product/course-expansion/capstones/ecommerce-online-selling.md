**Draft educational asset. Status: NOT REVIEWED / NOT PUBLISHED.**

**Outcome:** List products accurately and plan simple order and support workflows
**Practical objective:** Create a sample product catalogue and order-handling plan

## Fictional scenario

Prepare a ten-item fictional stationery store catalogue with realistic category names. No actual customers or real transaction records will be used.

## Starting materials and constraints

- Illustrative products: notebooks, folders, pens, markers, labels
- Cost of a sample notebook is PKR 180 and its sample sale price PKR 270; exclusions such as fees must be explained
- A fictional order requests two out-of-stock items

## Student deliverables

- Ten-product catalogue with SKU, title, description, sample price and stock state
- Simple stock-control sheet with reorder flags
- Transparent unit margin calculation with exclusions
- Order-to-dispatch process and customer notification templates
- Returns, damaged-goods and out-of-stock handling checklist

## Self-check rubric

- Product listings make no unsupported quality claims
- Unit margin and pricing math are correctly labeled
- Stock controls avoid pretending unavailable goods can ship
- Customer support promises follow documented business rules
- Private contact and payment details stay out of sample data
