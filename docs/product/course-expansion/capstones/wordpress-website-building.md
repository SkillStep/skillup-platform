## Fictional scenario

Create a sandbox website for fictional Mira Repairs. It must be clear on mobile, accessible, and safe for contact enquiries; no real payment collection or customer records are permitted.

## Starting materials and constraints

- Pages: Home, Services, About, Contact, Privacy Notice
- Key service categories: inspection, repair consultation, maintenance booking
- Fictional contact details only; actual business location and pricing remain unverified

## Student deliverables

- Page sitemap and navigation hierarchy
- Draft Home and Services page copy with meaningful headings
- Sandbox page layouts including a responsive contact call to action
- Contact form field list with consent and spam-protection plan
- Backup, performance, accessibility and launch-readiness checklist

## Self-check rubric

- Navigation and primary action are obvious
- Website does not invent company facts or prices
- Contact form collects only necessary information
- Mobile layout, labels and keyboard use are tested
- No public launch without ownership, legal and privacy review
