**Draft educational asset. Status: NOT REVIEWED / NOT PUBLISHED.**

**Outcome:** Plan safe AI-assisted workflows, verify outputs and document accountability
**Practical objective:** Design a repeatable AI-assisted task workflow with human checks

## Fictional scenario

Design a safe AI-assisted meeting-summary workflow from fictional notes. Human review is mandatory before the summary is circulated; do not feed real client notes, confidential records or personal data to an unapproved provider.

## Starting materials and constraints

- Fictional meeting notes: a website update needs a QA owner, a Friday deadline and sign-off
- One deliberately ambiguous item: 'finish the design soon'
- A prior AI summary incorrectly claims the launch is approved

## Student deliverables

- Before/after workflow map with explicit human checkpoints
- Reusable prompt template specifying scope, format, source constraints and uncertainty handling
- Verification checklist for names, deadlines, approval and cited evidence
- Demonstration identifying the false approval claim and replacing it with an uncertainty note
- Short comparison of time and correction effort using synthetic examples

## Self-check rubric

- AI never fabricates authority, facts or approvals
- Ambiguous notes remain unresolved unless a source confirms them
- Sensitive materials are excluded by design
- The workflow can be tested repeatably with fictional inputs
- Human final accountability is clear

## Ethical and technical boundaries

Use fictional or self-created source material only. Do not collect real customer records, upload sensitive personal information, scrape copyrighted courses, fabricate employment claims or invent commercial performance. Where a task requires a third-party product or an editor, provide a no-cost planning alternative. Do not claim professional assessment or accredited certification.
