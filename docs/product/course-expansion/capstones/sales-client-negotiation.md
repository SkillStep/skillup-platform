# Sales & Client Negotiation — practical capstone project

**Draft educational asset. Status: NOT REVIEWED / NOT PUBLISHED.**

**Outcome:** Discover client needs, propose fairly and handle objections with evidence
**Practical objective:** Prepare a discovery script, proposal and follow-up sequence

## Fictional scenario

Create a transparent creative-services offer for fictional Beacon Retail. The client requests eight social posts and two short reels within one month; scope, prices and revisions must be explicit rather than persuasive by pressure.

## Starting materials and constraints

- Fictional base scope: 8 posts, 2 reels, one approval round
- Potential add-on: one additional day of product photography
- The client asks for daily revisions but has not specified a change process

## Student deliverables

- Discovery question list focused on audience, approvals, rights and objectives
- Two-option proposal with deliverables, assumptions and exclusions
- Clear approval and revision policy with escalation path
- Three follow-up messages for budget, scope and deadline objections
- Commercial handover checklist before production begins

## Self-check rubric

- No fabricated client evidence, scarcity or guaranteed results
- Deliverable counts, costs and revision boundaries are unambiguous
- Responses are respectful and avoid manipulative language
- Dependencies and copyright responsibilities are visible
- Handover describes what constitutes accepted work
