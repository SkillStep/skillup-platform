# Video Editing for Reels — practical capstone project

**Draft educational asset. Status: NOT REVIEWED / NOT PUBLISHED.** 
**Course:** `video-editing-reels` · **Wave 2** · **Target:** 24 proposed levels.
**Outcome:** Plan, cut and export short videos that communicate one clear idea
**Practical objective:** Produce a 30-second original promotional video

## Fictional scenario

Create an original 30-second promotional reel for a fictional reusable notebook. Record your own footage or use rights-cleared, explicitly licensed visuals; no copied ads or copyrighted music.

## Starting materials and constraints

- Audience: students, one clear notebook benefit, vertical mobile screen
- Footage: close-up product, writing demonstration, finished notes, original title card
- Minimum accessibility: readable captions for any speech and a voice-free option

## Student deliverables

- Six-shot storyboard with a clear hook, middle and call to action
- A 30-second original edit with stable framing and nonmisleading product claims
- Caption transcript and safe-zone/legibility checklist
- Asset-and-sound rights inventory
- Playback QA note covering sound levels, format, aspect ratio and device checks

## Self-check rubric

- The message is intelligible without relying on tiny subtitles
- Cuts and transitions support story continuity instead of distracting
- Rights and source ownership are documented
- Exports preserve aspect ratio and meaningful content
- No fabricated product performance claims
