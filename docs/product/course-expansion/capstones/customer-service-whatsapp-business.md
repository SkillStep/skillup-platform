**Outcome:** Respond to customers clearly and resolve issues consistently
**Practical objective:** Prepare a customer service response and escalation playbook

## Fictional scenario

Fictional online shop Example Cart receives a delayed order, a product query, a billing complaint and an exchange request. No real phone numbers, payment details or customer conversations may be used.

## Starting materials and constraints

- Order EX-104: estimated dispatch Tuesday; supplier delay confirmed Wednesday; confirmed dispatch now Friday
- Customer asks for a refund, but company policy and payment settlement status are unknown
- Business operating hours: weekdays 9:00–18:00 Pakistan Standard Time

## Student deliverables

- Twelve helpful WhatsApp Business quick-reply templates classified by inquiry type
- Three specific scripts for EX-104: acknowledgement, verified update and internal escalation
- Escalation matrix showing the responsible role and when escalation is required
- Simple ticket tracker fields that avoid unnecessary personal data
- An accuracy-and-tone checklist for refunds, delays and public complaints

## Self-check rubric

- Replies distinguish verified status from unknown or pending investigation
- No invented compensation, refund guarantee, shipping date or policy
- Customer dignity and de-escalation are maintained
- Escalation ownership and follow-up timing are explicit
- Private order or payment data is never disclosed in public messages
