## Fictional scenario

Prepare a dashboard from fictional weekly orders and customer-support data. The data has one duplicate transaction and one unresolved date; learners must document these rather than silently discard evidence.

## Starting materials and constraints

- Week 1: 100 orders, 4 complaints; week 2: 125 orders, 3 complaints
- Week 3: 120 orders, 6 complaints; week 4: 140 orders, 2 complaints
- An audit log states that one exported order ID may have appeared twice

## Student deliverables

- Clean synthetic dataset with explicit change/reconciliation notes
- Three defined KPIs, including an order-weighted complaint measure
- Two appropriately chosen, clearly labeled charts
- Brief interpretation with one meaningful limitation
- One-page dashboard containing one carefully qualified operational recommendation

## Self-check rubric

- Totals, denominators and units are accurate
- Potential duplicates are investigated against source IDs
- Charts avoid clipped scales that exaggerate differences
- Conclusions do not claim unsupported causes
- A reviewer can reproduce the result from supplied values
