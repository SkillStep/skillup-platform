# Presentation & Public Speaking — practical capstone project

**Draft educational asset. Status: NOT REVIEWED / NOT PUBLISHED.** 
**Course:** `presentation-public-speaking` · **Wave 2** · **Target:** 24 proposed levels.
**Outcome:** Explain ideas clearly, design useful slides and handle questions
**Practical objective:** Prepare and rehearse a three-minute presentation

## Fictional scenario

Deliver an original three-minute briefing titled 'How to reduce avoidable email loops in a student project team', using fictional experiences, accessible slides and honest examples.

## Starting materials and constraints

- Audience: 12 undergraduate students with mixed presentation experience
- Time: three minutes including a 20-second opening and short close
- Supporting evidence is fictional; avoid external statistics unless cited

## Student deliverables

- One-sentence audience promise and a three-part talk outline
- Five-slide deck with meaningful headings and accessible visuals
- Speech notes or a short transcript
- Two likely audience questions with defensible responses
- Practice self-evaluation using timing, clarity and audience comprehension

## Self-check rubric

- Message, visual hierarchy and speech support one coherent goal
- Talk fits the time limit without excessive speed
- Slides can be understood without relying on color alone
- Questions are answered without fabricating facts
- Feedback prompts lead to concrete improvements
