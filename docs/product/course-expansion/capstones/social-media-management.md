# Social Media Management — practical capstone project

**Draft educational asset. Status: NOT REVIEWED / NOT PUBLISHED.** 
**Course:** `social-media-management` · **Wave 1** · **Target:** 24 proposed levels.
**Outcome:** Plan content, handle communities and measure meaningful outcomes
**Practical objective:** Prepare a 14-day channel calendar and response plan

## Fictional scenario

Fictional Naya Brew wants to attract local weekend breakfast customers. The brand has an unverified 14-day social content budget; the student must plan realistic content and explain the limits of forecasted engagement.

## Starting materials and constraints

- Platforms: Instagram and Facebook posts/reels, TikTok reels only
- Operational constraint: team can shoot one morning per week and review content within two days
- Sample previous week: 12,000 impressions, 700 interactions, 45 relevant enquiries; fictional sample only

## Student deliverables

- Clear audience and goal statement with one outcome KPI
- 14-day publishing calendar with creative brief, format, platform and owner for each item
- Three original post concepts, two reel concepts and caption ideas
- Ten customer-response templates, including a complaint and privacy escalation
- One-page report separating impressions, engagement and verified enquiries

## Self-check rubric

- The calendar respects real production/approval capacity and platform-specific formats
- Every piece has a reason to exist and an intended audience
- No guaranteed growth, made-up sales attribution or artificial urgency
- Support replies protect private details and identify escalation ownership
- Report calculations define denominators and relevant periods
