# Capstone — Digital Safety and Incident-Response Plan

**Course:** Cybersecurity & Digital Safety · **Status:** Unreviewed AI-assisted original draft · **Publication:** forbidden

## Fictional organization
**Riverview Community Library** operates one shared public tablet, two staff accounts, a volunteer registration document and a public event page. All scenarios below are simulated. Use **no actual passwords, real URLs, account IDs, SMS codes or personal records**.

## Four scenario cards
**A — Fake prize SMS:** "You've won a community grant; send PKR 1,500 by midnight." Caller is unknown. Use a fictional sender only; do not reproduce a clickable malicious URL.

**B — Unexpected MFA request:** A library staff member receives an authentication push notification while not attempting to sign in.

**C — Overshared registration file:** A fictional spreadsheet with names and volunteer shift preferences was mistakenly assigned public-edit access.

**D — Lost work tablet:** The staff tablet is missing after an event, and the team does not know which services are still signed in.

## Work
1. Build a **risk register** with risk, possible harm, affected information, likelihood assumption, priority and responsible role. Never identify a real person.
2. Write **ten preventive controls** covering passwords/passphrases, MFA, recovery codes, patching, minimum permissions, secure sharing, backups and phishing reporting.
3. Produce four **step-by-step incident playbooks** covering immediate containment, verified recovery, evidence preservation, internal escalation and communication without spreading malicious links.
4. For card C, specify how to revoke public edit access and assess the categories of exposed information. Do not promise that changing link permissions erases previously downloaded copies.
5. For card D, state which sessions/devices should be reviewed or revoked and how to verify recovery.
6. Write a short notice to staff distinguishing **confirmed facts** from **unknown facts**. Do not fabricate incident scope.

## Deliverable
One 4–6 page plan with a one-page risk register, preventive checklist, four incident playbooks, responsibilities and a monthly review reminder (written into the plan; no automation is created).

## Rubric (self-review, subject expert required before release)
| Criterion | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| Risks prioritized and matched to realistic controls | 0–2 |
| Defensive responses in all four cases are safe and specific | 0–2 |
| Account/MFA/recovery and least-privilege guidance is accurate | 0–2 |
| Evidence and uncertainty are handled without oversharing | 0–2 |
| Roles, verification checks and review cycle are usable | 0–2 |

**Suggested project benchmark:** 8/10; this is not an approved certificate passing threshold. Human cybersecurity review required; no offensive instructions or live phishing tests.
