# Capstone — Consultative Client Offer Pack

**Course identity:** `sales-client-negotiation` (reused); learner title **Sales, Negotiation & Client Management** · **Status:** AI-assisted draft, independent review pending · **Publication:** forbidden

## Fictional client brief
**Northwind Bakery** (synthetic) wants an introductory four-week social-content programme. It has a **PKR 120,000 provisional service budget**, two existing product photos and one person available to approve assets. Sales and customer acquisition outcomes have not been established. The bakery may provide additional product images if asked; paid media and location filming have **not** been authorized.

## Work
1. Draft an eight-question discovery script covering goal, audience, current channels, target action, available creative assets, approvals, intended launch date, budget boundaries and success measurement.
2. Write a *confirmed vs assumed vs open* requirements recap. Keep unknown decision-maker details, exact launch date and campaign KPI targets marked as questions.
3. Present **two feasible options** using the same scope table. Example fictional options (edit transparently, no invented external quotations): **Basic** — 4 static posts and 2 short edited clips, one review round, **PKR 80,000**. **Extended** — 6 static posts and 4 edited clips, two review rounds, **PKR 120,000**. State whether taxes apply as **unconfirmed**, and show that filming and paid ads are **excluded from both**.
4. Show dependencies: client asset handover, brief confirmation, first draft, revisions and approved posting plan. Do not promise a launch date until inputs are confirmed.
5. Write two objections and respectful responses: "Your package is beyond our budget" and "Can you add three reels at no extra charge?" Offer scope trade-offs or a written change order rather than pressure.
6. Draft a short follow-up email or message referencing the options and a client handover checklist for delivery staff.
7. Audit the final proposal for false scarcity, unsupported ROI guarantees, hidden fees, unlicensed examples and contradictory exclusions.

## Deliverables
Discovery worksheet, requirement recap, two-option commercial comparison, four-week production schedule **with conditional dates**, objections and responses, polite follow-up, and internal project-manager handover.
